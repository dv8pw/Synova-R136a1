With your authorization, the application accesses Google Drive file contents and related information through the Google Drive API to read and update files for synchronization with your personal application environment. File data may be stored in that environment as needed for synchronization and retained only as long as needed for this purpose. It is shared only as needed with services involved in the authorized synchronization, at your direction, or when legally required. Google Drive file data is not sold or used for advertising.

We do not sell personal information or use Google user data for advertising. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the services, disclosed when legally required, or shared at your direction.

Storage and Security

Data and authentication tokens may be stored where needed for authorized functionality. We use reasonable measures to protect stored information and retain it only as long as necessary for the stated purposes or as legally required. Hosting and other service providers may process data in other countries, subject to applicable safeguards.

Your Choices and Rights

You can revoke an application's Google access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access prevents future authorized API access but does not automatically delete data already stored.

Contact us using the email above to request access, correction, or deletion of applicable personal data, or to exercise other rights available under data protection law.
